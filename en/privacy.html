Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

In short

  • Your company's data stays on your computers, or in a team cloud that you own. We can't see it.
  • Solar Sizer has no analytics, no advertising and no account with us.
  • Once a day the program asks GitHub whether a new version exists. Nothing about your company is sent.
  • We only receive what you choose to send us by WhatsApp or email.

Who we are

Solar Sizer is a Windows program for solar companies, and this website offers its free trial. Questions about this policy go to the seller by WhatsApp (+964 784 373 1036) or email (hamjdh27@gmail.com).

The program on your computers

Your company's data

Products, prices, costs, stock, quotes, customers and settings are saved on each computer in %APPDATA%\SolarSizer. They are not sent to us. Backups you make with Export backup… are files you keep.

The team cloud (optional)

If your manager turns on the team cloud, your data is also kept in one place that your company chooses and controls. We have no access to any of them.

  • A shared folder: Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox, or a folder on your office server. The data is stored as files in that folder, and the folder's service (Google, Microsoft or Dropbox) keeps it under its own privacy policy. Anyone who can open the folder can read every file in it, including costs. Roles in Solar Sizer only decide what each person sees on screen. Passwords are stored in the folder only as salted hashes, never as text.
  • Supabase: a project your company creates in its own Supabase account. You choose its region and who can join, and its database rules stop staff from reading what their role may not see. Supabase's own privacy policy covers how it stores that data.
  • Your own server: the same database as Supabase, on a computer in your office. The data doesn't leave your company.

Your manager can move the company from one place to another. Solar Sizer copies the data straight from the old place to the new one, and keeps a backup file on the manager's computer. Nothing passes through us. The old place keeps its copy until your company deletes it.

Your customers' details

Customer names, phone numbers and locations that you type into quotes stay in your data and your team cloud. Your company decides how they are used, and is responsible for them.

Checking for updates

Once a day, Solar Sizer asks GitHub (api.github.com) for the newest version, and downloads it from GitHub if you click Update now. Like any website, GitHub sees your computer's IP address. The request also says which version of Solar Sizer you have. No company data, licence or personal details are sent. GitHub's privacy statement covers what GitHub keeps.

Your licence

Licence keys are checked on your computer, without the internet. A key contains your company name, its end date and the number of users. We keep a record of each key we make (company name, dates and number of users) so we can renew it and help you.

Reports by email

If you turn on emailed reports, they are sent through your own company email account, to the addresses you enter. They don't pass through us.

Feedback, help and buying

When you click Report a problem / Send feedback in Help, or Buy / renew by WhatsApp, Solar Sizer opens WhatsApp or your email program with a message ready. You see it before you send it, and nothing is sent until you press Send. If Include technical details is ticked (it is by default, and you can untick it), the message also contains your Solar Sizer and Windows versions, your company name, whether the team cloud is on and your role in it, and recent error messages. We use what you send only to answer you, fix problems and handle your licence.

This website

  • It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub receives your IP address when you visit, as with any website.
  • The text font is loaded from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address too.
  • The download button asks GitHub for the newest installer.
  • The site sets no cookies, has no forms and uses no analytics or advertising.
  • The Buy button opens WhatsApp. Messages there are covered by WhatsApp's own policy.

How long we keep things

Messages you send us stay in our WhatsApp or email for as long as we need them to support you. Licence records are kept while your licence or its renewals are active, and for our sales records after that.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you or your company, and ask us to correct or delete it, by WhatsApp or email. Deleting a licence record means we can't renew that licence for you.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top.
