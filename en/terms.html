Terms of sale and licence

Last updated: 5 November 2026

In short

  • Try every feature free for 14 days before you pay.
  • A licence is for one company for one year, with every update during that year.
  • Your company's data is yours. We never see it, and it is never deleted when a licence ends.
  • Solar Sizer helps your engineers design. They still check every system before it is installed.

Who sells Solar Sizer

Solar Sizer is sold by its developer. To buy, renew or ask about these terms, contact us by WhatsApp (+964 784 373 1036) or email (hamjdh27@gmail.com).

The free trial

Solar Sizer works for 14 days without a licence, with every feature. Quotes made during the trial are marked "TRIAL VERSION". When the trial ends, the program still opens and shows your data, but saving quotes, printing and PDFs stop until a licence key is entered.

What a licence gives you

  • The right for one company to use Solar Sizer until the end date in its licence key, usually one year after purchase.
  • Use on as many of the company's own computers as it needs. With the team cloud, the licence also sets how many people the manager can approve.
  • Every update published before the end date.
  • Your company name, as written in the key, printed on every quote as "Licensed to …".

Paying and receiving your key

The price is the one we agree with you before you pay. You pay by the method we agree together, such as a wallet transfer, bank transfer or cash. Once we have confirmed the payment, we send your licence key by WhatsApp or email. Enter it in Solar Sizer under Help → Licence…; nothing needs reinstalling.

Please give us the company name exactly as it should be printed on your quotes. If it is wrong in the key, tell us and we will send a corrected key.

Renewing

Solar Sizer reminds you from 30 days before the end date. To renew, pay for the next year and we send a new key, which you paste over the old one. For 14 days after the end date everything still works, with a warning. After that, saving quotes, printing and PDFs stop until a new key is entered. Your products, quotes and settings are kept.

What you may not do

  • Give, sell or lend your licence key to another company, or let another company use Solar Sizer under your licence.
  • Change, copy or take apart the program, or try to get around the licence check.
  • Remove or hide the "Licensed to …" or "TRIAL VERSION" mark on quotes.

If a key is shared or misused, we may refuse to renew it.

Refunds

Because you can try every feature free for 14 days first, payments are not refunded once your licence key has been sent. If the key does not work, contact us and we will fix it or send a new one.

Your data

Your products, prices, quotes and customers belong to your company. They are kept on your computers and, if you use it, in your own team cloud. We cannot see them. Keep regular backups (Settings → Backup). The team cloud runs where your company chooses: a shared folder (Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox or your office server), a Supabase project in your company's own account, or your own server. The terms of that service apply to it. A shared folder is the simpler choice, but anyone who can open it can read every file in it, including costs. If staff must never see costs, use Supabase or your own server. See the privacy policy for details.

Designs, prices and responsibility

Solar Sizer designs from the products and values your company enters, and refuses a design when a value it needs is missing. It is a design and quoting aid, not a replacement for a qualified engineer. A qualified person in your company must check every design, the equipment's real datasheets and the site before installation. Prices on quotes come from the prices you enter.

We work to keep Solar Sizer correct and fix the problems you report. We are not responsible for losses caused by wrong product data, by a design that was not checked, or by using the program outside these terms. In any case, our responsibility is limited to the amount you paid for the current licence year.

Support

While your licence or trial is active, we answer questions and problem reports by WhatsApp or email. Help → Report a problem / Send feedback prepares a message for you.

Changes

If these terms change, we update this page and the date at the top. The terms you bought under apply until the end of that licence year.
