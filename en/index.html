Safe designs

Designs your crew can install with confidence

Solar Sizer checks every system against the real limits of the equipment, and refuses a design rather than guess.

  • Voltage on cold mornings. Panels give more voltage in the cold. Strings are sized so they stay under the inverter's maximum even then.
  • Current per input. A panel whose current is above the inverter's limit is never paired with it.
  • No guessing. If a product is missing a value the design needs, it says which one, instead of assuming.
  • Ready for the site. The work order shows the strings, the battery wiring and the row spacing.
From a real work order: longest string ≈ 296 V, up to 331 V on cold mornings; inverter max 500 V.

Questions

Good to know

What does the free trial include?

Everything, for 14 days. Quotes made during the trial are marked "TRIAL VERSION". Your products, quotes and settings are kept when you activate a licence.

Does it need the internet?

No. On one computer it works fully offline and keeps your data on that computer. The team cloud needs the internet to share quotes and stock between computers, and keeps working offline until the connection returns.

Which computers does it run on?

Windows 10 or Windows 11, 64-bit. Nothing else needs to be installed.

Will it work with the products I sell?

Yes. Solar Sizer has no fixed product list. It designs only from the products and prices you enter, from any brand.

Who can see my cost prices?

Managers, and the roles the manager allows. Salesmen see selling prices, and quotes sent to customers never contain your costs. For the strictest privacy use Supabase or your own server: on a shared folder, anyone who can open the folder can read its files.

What if my computer breaks?

Solar Sizer keeps a backup of your data every day, and keeps the last 14. With the team cloud, every computer also keeps a copy, and your own office server can take its own daily backups.

How do updates work?

Solar Sizer checks once a day. When a new version is ready you see what has changed and an Update now button. Your data and licence are kept.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is that safe?

Yes. Windows shows this for new programs. Click "More info", then "Run anyway". Every update is checked against our signature before it installs.
